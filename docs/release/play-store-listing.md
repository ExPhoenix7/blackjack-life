## Full Description

Blackjack Life combines classic blackjack with a simple money progression loop.

Play hands, place bets, manage your credit, unlock achievements, and grow your in-game lifestyle through store purchases, real estate income, vehicles, items, and the Money Machine.

Features:

- Classic blackjack gameplay
- Responsive mobile-first table layout
- Multiple in-game accounts
- Store with properties, vehicles, and items
- Money Machine tap and passive income upgrades
- Rental income from real estate
- Achievements and profile progress
- Optional rewarded ads for bonus in-game credit

Blackjack Life uses virtual in-game credit only. The game does not offer real-money gambling, cash prizes, or real-world value payouts.

## Data Safety Draft

Use this as a Play Console draft, then verify against the current Google Play form before submission.

### Does the app collect or share user data?

Yes, because Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares data for ads, analytics, diagnostics, and fraud prevention.

### Data types likely applicable because of Google Mobile Ads SDK

- Location: approximate location inferred from IP address
- App activity: app interactions, taps, ad interactions, video views
- App info and performance: diagnostics, crash/performance information
- Device or other IDs: advertising ID, app set ID, device/account identifiers

### Purposes

- Advertising or marketing
- Analytics
- Fraud prevention, security, and compliance
- App functionality for rewarded ads

### Encryption

Data collected by Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit according to Google's SDK disclosure.

### Deletion

Local game progress can be deleted by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app. Privacy questions can be sent to `couzeensdev@gmail.com`.
