## Full Description

Blackjack Life is a blackjack card game with a virtual money progression loop.

Play hands, place virtual bets, manage your credit, unlock achievements, and grow your in-game lifestyle through store purchases, real estate income, vehicles, items, and the Money Machine.

Features:

- Play classic blackjack hands with virtual credit
- Place bets, hit, stand, and chase 21
- Manage up to 3 in-game accounts
- Buy properties, vehicles, and lifestyle items
- Upgrade the Money Machine for tap and passive income
- Collect rental income from real estate
- Track achievements and profile progress
- Watch optional rewarded ads for bonus virtual credit

Blackjack Life uses virtual in-game credit only. The app does not offer real-money gambling, cash prizes, cash-out features, crypto rewards, or any real-world value payout.

Rewarded ads are optional and only provide bonus virtual credit inside the game.

### Collects or shares user data?

Yes, because Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares data for ads, analytics, diagnostics, and fraud prevention.

### Data Types To Declare Because Of Google Mobile Ads SDK

- Approximate location: collected or inferred from IP address by Google Mobile Ads SDK.
- App interactions: collected by Google Mobile Ads SDK for ad interaction and measurement.
- Other user-generated content: no.
- Financial info: no.
- Health and fitness: no.
- Messages: no.
- Photos and videos: no.
- Audio files or voice: no.
- Files and docs: no.
- Calendar: no.
- Contacts: no.
- App info and performance: diagnostics, crash, and performance information may be collected by Google Mobile Ads SDK.
- Device or other IDs: advertising ID, app set ID, and device/account identifiers may be collected by Google Mobile Ads SDK.

### Purposes To Declare

- Advertising or marketing
- Analytics
- Fraud prevention, security, and compliance
- App functionality for rewarded ads

### Sharing

The app does not share local game progress with a Couzeens backend. Advertising-related data may be collected and shared by Google Mobile Ads SDK as described by Google's SDK disclosure.

### Encryption

Data collected by Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit according to Google's SDK disclosure.

### Deletion

Local game progress can be deleted by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app. Privacy questions can be sent to `couzeensdev@gmail.com`.
