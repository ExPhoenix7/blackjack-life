Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Blackjack Life is a mobile blackjack and money management game.

Developer: Couzeens
Privacy contact: couzeensdev@gmail.com
App package: com.exphoenix.blackjacklife

Data We Store On Your Device

Blackjack Life stores game progress locally on your device, including:

  • Account names created inside the game
  • Credit balance
  • Store purchases
  • Money Machine progress
  • Rental income progress
  • Achievement progress
  • Sound setting

This local game data is stored on your device using the app's local storage. Blackjack Life does not operate its own backend server and does not upload this local game progress to a developer-controlled server.

Advertising

Blackjack Life uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK to show rewarded ads.

Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share data for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, including:

  • IP address
  • User product interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad video views
  • Diagnostic information, such as app and SDK performance information
  • Device and account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID

Google states that data collected by Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

Learn more: Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure and Google privacy policy.

Permissions

Blackjack Life does not request access to camera, contacts, location, microphone, photos, or files.

The Android build may include audio-related system permission through Expo Audio / React Native audio handling. This is used for game sound behavior, not for recording audio.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may include the Android advertising ID permission for advertising and fraud-prevention purposes.

How We Use Data

Local game data is used to:

  • Save and restore progress
  • Show current balance and owned items
  • Track achievements
  • Keep game settings

Advertising data handled by Google Mobile Ads SDK is used by Google and its partners for:

  • Showing ads
  • Rewarded ad functionality
  • Ad measurement and reporting
  • Fraud prevention
  • SDK diagnostics

Sharing

Blackjack Life does not sell local game progress.

Advertising-related data may be collected and shared by Google Mobile Ads SDK as described in Google's documentation and policies.

Retention And Deletion

Local game progress remains on your device until you delete it, clear app storage, or uninstall the app.

If Blackjack Life later adds cloud accounts, login, or backend storage, this policy must be updated before release.

Children's Privacy

Blackjack Life is not intended for children. Do not list the app as child-directed unless the app, store listing, SDK configuration, and Google Play declarations are reviewed for child-directed policy requirements.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The updated date at the top of this policy should be changed whenever the policy is updated.
